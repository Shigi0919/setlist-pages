Data Deletion

Setlist / Provided by Shigi Studio

The Setlist app does not require an account. Your setlists, songs, and other data you create are stored only on your device and are never sent to any external server.

How to request deletion of your data

  1. Open the contact form using the button below.
  2. Write “Data deletion request” in the message.
  3. Include an email address we can reply to, then submit the form.
  4. We will delete the data within 30 days of receiving your request and confirm by email.

Open the contact form

If you cannot use the form, you may also email us at shigi.studio.app.support@gmail.com.

What is deleted or retained, and for how long

Setlists, songs, and your other content Never sent off your device. This data is stored only on your device.
You can delete it inside the app, and uninstalling the app removes it from your device completely. The developer does not hold a copy, so no request is needed.
Usage analytics Anonymous event data about which features are used and how often (via PostHog). It contains no name, email address, or other information that identifies you.
Retention: up to 1 year from collection, after which it is deleted automatically. To have it deleted sooner, follow the steps above.
Advertising data Google AdMob collects an advertising ID, IP address, and similar data to serve ads. This data is held by Google and retained under the Google Privacy Policy.
You can reset or delete your advertising ID at any time in your device settings (Android: Settings → Google → Ads / iOS: Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking).

Account deletion

The app has no sign-up or login, so there is no account to delete.
